1. Executive Summary & Vision

AvanzaMath is an equity-centered, dual-language, gamified elementary math learning platform designed for elementary students in grades K–5 from low-income, predominantly Hispanic neighborhoods.

Instead of abstract, disconnected drills, AvanzaMath anchors arithmetic (Addition, Subtraction, Multiplication, Division) in familiar neighborhood cultural traditions: counting fresh fruits at El Puesto de Frutas, baking conchas at La Panadería, calculating change at El Mercado, and dividing treats from La Piñata.

🌟 Core Pillars of AvanzaMath:
  • Culturally Resonant Contexts: Everyday scenarios Hispanic elementary kids immediately recognize.
  • Bilingual Segmented Toggle [ 🇲🇽 ES | 🇺🇸 EN ]: Instant dual-language switching with native Latin American Spanish and English TTS audio.
  • Zero-Friction Student Onboarding: Avatar companion selection and 4-word anonymous recovery codes (zero student PII required).
  • Family Math Center: Spanish-first parent progress tracking, zero-password QR pairing, and WhatsApp shareable progress cards.

2. Problem Statement & Demographic Context

  • Curriculum Disconnection: Standard word problems rely on unfamiliar cultural contexts rather than real-world community life.
  • Language Friction for Dual-Language Learners: ELL students often understand math logic but struggle with English-heavy standardized phrasing.
  • Digital & Hardware Constraints: Low-income families often rely on prepaid data or shared devices with intermittent connectivity.
  • Parental Barriers: Spanish-speaking parents want to help with math but face complex, English-only login portals.

4. Curriculum & Pedagogical Framework

AvanzaMath implements the Concrete-Pictorial-Abstract (CPA) approach combined with Culturally Responsive Teaching (CRT).

Level Grade Arithmetic Domain Core Competencies Hispanic Cultural Theme Module
Nivel 1 K – 1st Number Sense & Add/Sub Counting 0–20, Ten-Frames, Number Bonds, Single-digit (+ / −) El Puesto de Frutas: Counting mangos, avocados, papayas, and oranges on ten-frames.
Nivel 2 2nd – 3rd Multi-digit Add/Sub & Multiplication Regrouping, Arrays, Skip Counting, Times Tables (1–10) La Panadería: Baking conchas and empanadas in rows $\times$ cols; El Mercado: Dollars & coins ($1, $5, $10, $20, 25¢).
Nivel 3 4th – 5th Division & Equal Sharing Long Division, Factors, Multiples, Equivalent Fractions La Piñata: Fair candy sharing into party favor bags (*bolsitas*); La Cocina: Scaling family recipes.
Todos K – 5th Mental Math Review All 4 Operations (+, −, ×, ÷), Speed, Pattern Recognition Lotería Matemática: Math Bingo with bean stamps, operation filters (+, −, ×, ÷), and vocal card caller (*El Cantor*).

5. Hispanic Cultural Integration Modules ("El Sistema Nuestra Cultura")

🏪 Everyday Community & Celebration Quests

  • La Panadería de Don Carlos: Multiplication as baking arrays (3 rows of 4 conchas $= 12$).
  • El Mercado y Tiendita: Budgeting purchases and calculating exact change from $10 or $20 bills.
  • La Piñata de Cumpleaños: Dividing piñata candy batches equally into party bags (*bolsitas*).
  • Lotería Matemática: Mental math Bingo review across addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
  • Héroes STEM: Real-world missions inspired by astronaut Dr. Ellen Ochoa and calculus educator Jaime Escalante.

6. Functional Requirements & Feature Specifications

FR-01: Welcome Landing & Quick Lesson Prep Hub (Página de Bienvenida)

  • Dedicated welcome home view featuring *Tito el Colibrí* and direct CTAs to start math quests or play Lotería.
  • Quick Prep Lessons: Conceptual summaries and tips for Ten-Frames, Arrays, Money Math, and Division.
  • Curated Video Linkages: Direct links to Khan Academy (English and Spanish) and Math Antics video lessons.

FR-02: Student Onboarding, Identity & Avatar Profile System (Registro sin Fricción)

  • Zero-PII Compliance: No email, password, or student personal identifying information collected.
  • Avatar Selection: Students pick cultural companions (*Tito el Colibrí 🐦*, *Sol el Axolote 🦎*, *Luna la Mariposa 🦋*).
  • 4-Word Magic Recovery Code & QR Pasaporte: Anonymous code (e.g., Colibri-Pan-Sol-24) and printable QR passport to resume progress across school Chromebooks and home tablets.

FR-03: Interactive Digital Manipulatives

  • Ten-Frames with Matching Fruit Emojis: 🥭 mangos, 🥑 avocados, 🍊 oranges, 🍈 papayas for hands-on counting.
  • Panadería Baking Trays: Multi-flavor concha arrays (pink, chocolate, vanilla) for multiplication grids.
  • Cash Register & Coin Counter: Interactive dollar bills ($1, $5, $10, $20) and change calculation.

FR-04: Friendly Hispanic Mascot & "Ganas" Gamification

  • *Tito el Colibrí* provides cheerful growth-mindset coaching and cultural role model facts on every screen.
  • Non-Punitive Pedagogy: Incorrect answers trigger gentle scaffolding hints; no loss of lives or lockouts.
  • Practice earns **Puntos Ganas** (⭐) to celebrate effort and consistency.

FR-05: Instant Dual-Language Engine & Audio Synthesis

  • Segmented header toggle [ 🇲🇽 ES | 🇺🇸 EN ] with instant bilingual translation and state persistence.
  • Native Web Speech API text-to-speech with Latin American Spanish (es-MX, es-US, es-419) and English (en-US).
  • Symbol normalizer translates operators naturally (Spanish: + más, − menos, × por, ÷ entre; English: + plus, − minus, × times, ÷ divided by).
  • Dedicated Auto-Read toggle (🗣️ Auto: OFF by default) and manual 🔊 Read Aloud buttons.

FR-06: Offline-First Progressive Web App (PWA)

  • 100% of arithmetic practice, audio syntheses, and mini-games function without internet via Service Worker.
  • Network-First Caching Strategy: Delivers fresh live updates while providing offline fallback.
  • Total initial asset payload strictly < 2.5 MB for instant loading on budget mobile networks.

FR-07: Family Math Center (El Portal Familiar) & Parent-Child Association

  • Shared Home Device View: Direct access to mastery progress and Ganas Stars on shared family phones/tablets.
  • Zero-Password QR & Magic Link Pairing: Parents scan the child's QR code or enter their 4-word code (Colibri-Pan-Sol-24) to link their phone to a read-only progress dashboard.
  • WhatsApp Progress Sharing: Encodes achievements into a pre-filled celebratory WhatsApp message with voiceover link.
  • Retos en Familia: Weekly dinner-table or grocery store math prompts in Spanish.

11. Verification & Acceptance Criteria

  1. Accessibility: All buttons, inputs, and questions have explicit aria-label attributes and pass 100% WCAG 2.1 AA audits.
  2. Instant Language Switching: Segmented language switcher toggles between English and Español under $50\text{ms}$ with full persistence across page refreshes.
  3. 100% Offline Capability: Disconnecting Wi-Fi during a math quest allows full continuation of problem solving, animations, audio, and local streak tracking.
  4. COPPA / FERPA Compliance: Zero collection of student emails, passwords, or personal identifying data.
  5. Cultural Grounding: Every grade level includes distinct, everyday Hispanic cultural scenarios (La Panadería, El Mercado, La Piñata, Lotería, or Héroes STEM).
